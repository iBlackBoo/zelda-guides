关于海拉尔攻略站About Hyrule Guides

最后更新：2026 年Last updated: 2026

海拉尔攻略站（Hyrule Guides）是一个由《塞尔达传说》系列玩家个人业余维护的非官方攻略站。我们热衷于把游戏中零散的探索经验整理成清晰、可执行的图文攻略，帮助更多玩家轻松畅游海拉尔大陆。Hyrule Guides is an unofficial walkthrough site maintained by Zelda fans in their spare time. We love turning scattered in-game experiences into clear, actionable guides so more players can roam Hyrule with ease.

我们提供什么What We Offer

  • 主线与支线任务的分步图文攻略；Step-by-step guides for main and side quests;
  • 装备、料理、收集向的实用技巧；Practical tips on gear, cooking and collectibles;
  • 高清全要素地图等数字资源（见 资源页）。Digital resources like the HD full map (see the Resources page).
